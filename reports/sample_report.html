Section 6 · Coaching Notes

How to Unlock More Sales Momentum

The goal is not to make Nurlan a pressure-first seller. The goal is to add stronger commitment mechanics to a consultative, clarity-based selling style.
Coaching 1
Add cost-of-inaction questions
Instead of pushing harder, ask: “What happens if this stays unresolved for another quarter?”
Coaching 2
Move from explanation to commitment
After explaining value, ask: “Based on this, what would need to happen for this to move forward?”
Coaching 3
Strengthen competitive reframing
In competitive deals, practice reframing criteria, not only improving the explanation of value.
